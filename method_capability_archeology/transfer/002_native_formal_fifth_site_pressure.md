## Fifth-site additions

### Proof force is implication-directed

A checker/verifier/proof artifact has standing through the proposition it establishes under explicit assumptions and derivation. Successful execution is not itself the semantic force of the evidence.

### Proof-bearing evidence may be distributed

A formal historical claim may require source semantics, formal transformation/object, derivation, checker/proof, and explicit limits/falsifiers together.

### Formal correction can replace the searched object

The Challenge's provisional eight-pair dependency reconstruction was corrected to five pairs from exact source semantics. The correction changes the object downstream proof search operates on.

Strong formal branch eliminations coexist with `Research-object standing: none`. Proof strength is level-specific.
